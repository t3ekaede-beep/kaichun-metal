Used cars and engines for export, from Kaohsiung, Taiwan

Kai-Chun Metal (開群金屬行) is an auto recycling yard in Alian District, Kaohsiung. We supply whole used cars and used engines to overseas buyers, mostly Japanese brands.

Ask us on LINESee export stock

What we supply

Every export vehicle and engine goes through Taiwan's official non-stolen vehicle verification before it leaves the yard. We prepare the export paperwork.

1Whole used cars

Sedans, SUVs, vans and light trucks. Toyota, Honda, Nissan and Mitsubishi are the most common.

2Used engines

Mostly removed from vehicles dismantled in our yard. For export, the engine number must match the official registration record.

3Prices follow the market

Tell us the model, year and quantity. We quote per unit and agree payment and shipping terms with you.
